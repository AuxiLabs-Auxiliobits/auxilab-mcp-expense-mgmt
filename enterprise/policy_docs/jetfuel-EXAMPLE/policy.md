# JetFuel — Finance Policy (excerpt)

## Connectivity
- Wi-Fi / internet reimbursement is capped at **$120** per month.

## Travel
- Air travel: business class permitted for flights over 8 hours.
- Hotel reimbursement is capped at **$300** per night in tier-1 cities, **$200** elsewhere.

## Software / subscriptions
- Individual SaaS subscriptions over **$50/month** require manager pre-approval.
