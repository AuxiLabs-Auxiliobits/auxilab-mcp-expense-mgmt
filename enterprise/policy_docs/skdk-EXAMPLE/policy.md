# SKDK — Finance Policy (excerpt)

## Connectivity
- Home internet stipend is capped at **$75** per month.

## Meals
- Per-meal limit is **$60**; client meals up to **$120** with an attendee list.

## Travel
- Air travel: economy for flights under 5 hours; premium economy permitted otherwise.
- Ground transport (rideshare/taxi) reimbursed at cost with a receipt.
