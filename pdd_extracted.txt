ExpenseOps — Product Design Document (PDD)
Project Title
ExpenseOps — AI-Powered Expense Management MCP Server
1. Executive Summary
ExpenseOps is an AI-assisted Expense Management MCP Server designed to automate employee expense validation, policy compliance checking, duplicate detection, expense categorization, and finance review workflows.
The platform simulates a real enterprise finance automation product used by Accounts Payable (AP) and Finance Operations teams.
The project exposes reusable MCP-compatible tools that can be consumed by AI agents or enterprise workflows.
The solution aims to reduce manual expense review effort, improve compliance accuracy, detect suspicious claims, and provide finance teams with a modern analytics dashboard.
2. Problem Statement
Finance teams manually review hundreds of employee expense claims every month.
Current problems:
Manual policy validation is time-consuming.
Duplicate expense claims often go unnoticed.
Missing receipts create audit issues.
Expense categorization is inconsistent.
Managers spend excessive time reviewing routine claims.
Fraudulent or suspicious patterns are difficult to detect.
Existing systems are often rigid and difficult to configure.
The goal of ExpenseOps is to automate these validations and provide a reusable intelligence layer through MCP tools.

24. Conclusion
ExpenseOps demonstrates how AI-assisted automation and MCP-compatible tooling can modernize finance operations.
The project combines:
rule-based automation,
configurable business logic,
AI-assisted intelligence,
modern dashboard analytics,
and reusable MCP tools
into a realistic enterprise finance automation platform.
The solution is designed to simulate a production-grade expense compliance product while remaining lightweight, modular, and beginner-friendly for hackathon implementation.
