# Crispin — Finance Policy (excerpt)

## Connectivity
- Wi-Fi / internet reimbursement is capped at **$100** per month; any amount above $100 is rejected. (Inclusive: exactly $100 is allowed.)

## Travel
- Air travel must be **economy class** for flights under 6 hours.
- Hotel reimbursement is capped at **$250** per night.

## Client entertainment
- Client entertainment requires an **itemised receipt** and an **attendee list**.

## Prohibited
- Personal expenses, alcohol-only tabs, and fines/penalties are not reimbursable.
