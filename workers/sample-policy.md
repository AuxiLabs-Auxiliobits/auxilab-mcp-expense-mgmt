# Sample Agency Expense Policy (local dev)

## Air Travel
Employees may book economy-class airfare for business trips. Flights must be booked at
least 7 days in advance where practical. Business-class air travel requires prior manager
approval and is only permitted for flights longer than 6 hours. Always attach the airline
receipt or e-ticket itinerary showing the fare and travel dates.

## Per Diem and Meals
Daily meal allowance is capped at $75 per day while travelling. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Hotels
Hotel stays are reimbursable up to $250 per night. Anything above the cap needs written
manager approval before the trip.

## Receipts
Any single expense over $25 requires an itemized receipt. Missing receipts may delay or
block reimbursement.
